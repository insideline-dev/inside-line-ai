# Inside Line — Progress Report

## Overview

This week delivered **Data Gates** (Epic 113) — a new step that collects the required documents from founders before a deal goes into deep due-diligence analysis — together with a set of security fixes, reliability improvements, and workflow polish around it.

---

## New Features & Improvements

**1. Data Gates — document collection step (headline feature)**

- After a deal passes initial screening and is advanced toward due diligence, it now lands in a "Data Gates" holding state instead of immediately running the full analysis. The platform checks which key documents (e.g. pitch deck, financials) are still missing.
- An investor can **request the missing documents from the founder**, **skip the gate** to push the deal straight into analysis, or let it **auto-advance** automatically once all required documents are present.
- _Where to find it:_ Investor side — open the **Due Diligence / pipeline page** and select the **"Data Gates"** sub-tab. Admin side — the **Due Diligence dashboard**, same **"Data Gates"** sub-tab.
- _How to verify:_ Take a deal in Screening and advance it. Confirm it appears under **Data Gates** (not immediately in analysis). Click **Skip** on its card and confirm it moves to **Analyzed** and the list updates on its own without a manual refresh.

**2. Request documents from founders via Clara (our AI email assistant)**

- From a Data Gates card, an investor can ask Clara to email the founder for the missing documents. When the founder replies with attachments, Clara files the documents to the deal and re-checks completeness automatically; the deal then advances on its own.
- _Where to find it:_ The **"Request documents"** action on a Data Gates card.
- _How to verify:_ Click request on a deal that has a founder email on file and confirm the founder receives Clara's email. Reply with an attachment and confirm the document appears on the deal and the deal progresses. If no founder email is on file, confirm the app prompts for one inline instead of erroring.

**3. Auto-advance once documents are complete**

- If the required documents are already present (or arrive later), the deal moves into the due-diligence analysis automatically — no manual chasing.
- _Where to find it:_ Investor **Thesis settings**, Data Gates section (required document types + auto-advance toggle, on by default).
- _How to verify:_ Advance a deal that already has both the pitch deck and financials present and confirm it skips the waiting state and goes straight into analysis. Advance one missing a document and confirm it waits in Data Gates.

**4. Cleaner screening, separated from due diligence**

- Initial screening no longer blocks a deal over missing paperwork — that is now handled in the dedicated Data Gates step, keeping the early funnel faster and clearer.
- The Screening view and the Due Diligence view now each show only their own relevant pipeline stages, so they no longer overlap.
- _Where to find it:_ The **Screening** tab vs. the **Due Diligence** deal view.
- _How to verify:_ Open a deal still in screening from the Due Diligence area and confirm it is guided back to the Screening page rather than showing a due-diligence pipeline.

**5. Founder team data is now retained**

- The analysis now saves the founders and team members it discovers from decks and company websites onto the deal record, so the team roster persists instead of being lost after processing.
- _Where to find it:_ The team/founders section of a processed deal.
- _How to verify:_ Run a deck through analysis and confirm discovered founders appear on the deal afterward.

**6. Faster pitch-deck submission**

- When submitting a deck, the platform now auto-detects and pre-fills the funding stage, only asking the user to confirm when it is unsure.
- _Where to find it:_ The quick deck-submit flow on the investor pipeline page.
- _How to verify:_ Submit a deck where the stage is clear and confirm the stage is pre-filled; submit one where it is ambiguous and confirm it drops into a review step asking you to pick the stage.

**7. Workflow cleanup**

- Removed an empty "Engaged" placeholder stage that had no functionality yet, tidied the investor screening list layout, and temporarily hid the Calibration feature (paused pending a redesign).
- _Where to find it:_ Investor and admin stage navigation (no more "Engaged" tab); the admin investor view (no Calibration tab for now).
- _How to verify:_ Confirm there is no empty "Engaged" stage and no Calibration tab.

---

## Critical Bug & Security Fixes

**Security — cross-account document access closed (high severity)**

- Fixed a vulnerability where one investor could potentially reach another investor's deal documents by guessing IDs. Ownership is now enforced on every Data Gates action.
- _How to verify:_ Signed in as one investor, attempt to open another investor's deal by its ID and confirm access is denied.

**Privacy — private deals no longer leak into public matching**

- Investor-submitted private deals were mistakenly being included in public cross-matching; they now correctly default to private.
- _How to verify:_ Submit a private deal as an investor and confirm it does not surface in another investor's cross-matched results.

**No more double-processing**

- Fixed a timing issue where a deal could start its due-diligence analysis twice if two actions landed at once.
- _How to verify:_ Skip and auto-advance the same deal in quick succession and confirm the analysis pipeline runs only once.

**Documents uploaded mid-analysis are no longer missed**

- If a founder's documents arrived while an analysis was already running, they could be silently skipped. They are now always picked up and trigger a fresh extraction.
- _How to verify:_ Upload a document while a deal is processing and confirm it is included in the next analysis pass.

**Clara accuracy fixes**

- Clara no longer reports just-received documents as still missing, now uses the **correct investment thesis** when a deal matches more than one investor, and reliably records its own replies in the conversation log.
- _How to verify:_ As an admin, open a founder-submitted deal in Data Gates and confirm the required documents reflect the matched investor's thesis (not a generic default). Reply to a Clara request with documents and confirm Clara does not re-ask for them.

**Stability — backend boot and email-send crashes resolved**

- Resolved an internal startup crash in the Clara module and a case where non-email channels (e.g. WhatsApp) could break the email-send path.
- _How to verify:_ Confirm the backend starts cleanly with no module errors, and that document requests still send successfully.

---

## Summary

The platform now has a complete document-collection step between screening and due diligence, with Clara handling founder follow-ups and deals advancing automatically once documents are in. Several security and reliability issues were closed along the way, leaving the deal pipeline noticeably more robust.
